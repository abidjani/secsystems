The AssetGuard AI Advantage

The smarter way to upgrade your security

You don’t need more cameras, you just need smarter ones. While many security companies will try to sell you an entirely new suite of hardware, AssetGuard offers local businesses a cost-effective way forward.

Works With What You Have

AssetGuard AI is designed to sit on top of your current infrastructure. If you have IP cameras and a network connection, you’re ready to go. We integrate with your existing systems, adding AI insight without the expensive rip-and-replace price tag.

Smart, Targeted Detection

The biggest problem with standard motion detection is false alarms. If your phone pings every time a tree moves in the wind or a cat runs past, you’ll eventually stop looking at the alerts. AssetGuard AI uses advanced behavioral filters to ignore the noise and focus on real risks: intrusion, loitering and asset interference.

Real-Time Threat Detection

The system is trained to recognise specific threats, including unauthorised persons in restricted zones or suspicious vehicles. It provides the early detection needed to intervene or call authorities.

Instant Alerts

No more nasty surprises in the morning. When a high-priority risk is detected, you get an immediate notification on your smartphone or desktop. You see what the AI sees, as it happens.

Tracks Movement & Behaviour

Criminals often survey a property before they strike. AssetGuard AI identifies loitering, repeat visits and unusual activity patterns, allowing you to identify a threat during the planning phase, not the execution phase.

Protects Access Points

Monitor your gates and loading docks with precision. Our AI can detect tailgating (where a vehicle follows an authorised person through a gate) and breaches of restricted areas.

Monitors Assets & Equipment

High-value assets like fuel tanks, heavy machinery and stock yards are often prime targets. Our AI recognises interaction with these specific objects, alerting you if someone is touching your equipment after hours.
